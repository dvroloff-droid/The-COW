Sunday edition · November 8, 2026

The NFL takes Week 9 overseas

Cincinnati faces Atlanta at Madrid's Bernabéu Stadium, with kickoff set for 9:30 a.m. ET. It is an early start to a full Sunday slate. Tonight, Tampa Bay visits Chicago for an 8:20 p.m. ET matchup.

Availability can shift late in the week. Check the latest NFL injury report before kickoff for player designations across Sunday's games.

Saturday results add pressure to the CFP race

Week 10 delivered several high-profile results among ranked teams. Oregon's win over Ohio State stands out, while Utah, Alabama, and Texas also finished with statement victories.

  • Oregon 41, Ohio State 28
  • Utah 28, BYU 20
  • Alabama 31, LSU 13
  • Texas 34, Missouri 20

Those results reshape the weekend conversation, but the wider playoff picture still depends on the remaining games and the next rankings update. Follow the NCAA live scoreboard for the full slate.

What to watch next

Start with the Madrid opener, then keep an eye on the final inactive lists and the primetime matchup in Chicago. In college football, Saturday's results make every remaining ranked matchup matter that much more.
